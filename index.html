🐔 Chicken Tag VR

You're the chicken from your model, alone in the farm arena. Collect all 20 golden eggs as fast as you can — reach them with your arms, jump, flap and push off. Check yourself out in the mirror at the start!

VR (Quest / WebXR)

  • Hands = your controllers (or your bare hands with hand tracking). The chicken arms stretch and follow your hands anywhere you move them.
  • Gorilla-style push-off: shove your hands against the ground, walls and boxes to launch yourself.
  • Flap: in the air, swing both arms down hard (3 flaps, refill on landing).
  • Left stick walk · right stick snap-turn · A / X jump · with bare hands: pinch left thumb + index to jump.

PC

  • W A S D move · Shift sprint · Space jump, press again in the air to flap
  • Mouse look (click to capture) · arrow keys also turn
  • Left / right mouse = punch out your left / right wing-hand. Punch the ground or walls to push off like in VR.
  • Phone / tablet: left side drag = move, right side drag = look, on-screen buttons for jump and hands.
  • C third-person · R respawn · Enter restart round

Shop & cosmetics

  • Every golden egg pays 100 🪙 chicken money (tagging is gone along with the AI chickens).
  • The shop is the hole right of the mirror: touch an item to buy it (Fedora 200, Christmas Hat 250, Top Hat 300, Angel Wings 600).
  • Joined in November 2026? Your free 2027 Jacket is already waiting in the cosmetic holder.
  • The cosmetic holder has a green arrow button on each end — touch one to switch to the other bench.
  • The cosmetic holder is beside the mirror on the left: touch something you own to wear or remove it. Progress saves in this browser.
